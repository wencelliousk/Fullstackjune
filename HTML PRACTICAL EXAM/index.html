Welcome to EldoHub

EldoHub Tech and AI Infrastructure is a community-driven technology hub based in Eldoret, Kenya. We provide training, co-working space, and infrastructure for innovators, students, and developers working in software development, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies.

AI & Machine Learning Training

Hands-on bootcamps covering Python, machine learning, and practical AI tools.


Software Development Courses

Fullstack development programs including web, mobile, and cloud applications.


Co-Working Space

High-speed internet, power backup, and a quiet environment for developers and startups.


Mentorship & Networking

Connect with experienced developers, AI researchers, and industry professionals.


Cloud & Infrastructure Support

Guidance on cloud computing, servers, and deployment infrastructure for local businesses.
