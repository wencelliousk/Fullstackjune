Our History

EldoHub Tech and AI Infrastructure was founded by a group of young innovators in Eldoret who saw a need for accessible technology training and infrastructure in the region. What started as small weekend coding meetups has grown into a fully equipped hub offering structured courses, mentorship, and project incubation for students and entrepreneurs.

Over the years, EldoHub has partnered with local universities, technology companies, and government innovation programs to expand access to digital skills training across Uasin Gishu County and beyond.

Our Mission

To equip young people in Eldoret and the wider North Rift region with practical skills in software development, artificial intelligence, and digital infrastructure, enabling them to build solutions for local and global challenges.

Our Vision

To become the leading technology and AI innovation hub in the North Rift region, recognized for producing skilled, job-ready, and innovative tech talent.
